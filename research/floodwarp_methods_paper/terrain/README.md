# Terrain calculations

Each synthetic family uses the continuous equations in the Hancock qualification with independent bounded draws for slope, incision and phase. DEM samples use cell centres and represent elevation in metres. Fixed-geometry experiments copy the parent cell elevation when subdividing the hydraulic grid. The separate preprocessing diagnostic evaluates area-weighted DEM aggregation. The two coarsening operators serve different experiments.

Drainage uses an eight-neighbour priority flood seeded at exterior and nodata-adjacent cells. Choose the steepest lower neighbour on the filled surface; route ties through the earlier visited flood parent. Accumulate physical contributing area in reverse topological order. A channel candidate has contributing area at least the greater of 100 m² and 2% of active area. The width proxy uses the distance across the lowest quarter of a 30 m window's local relief at those candidate channels. Treat this estimate as topographic confinement. Surveyed channels, underground links and structures can differ. [Priority-Flood method](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.04463)

Horizontal and vertical gradients use centred differences internally and one-sided boundary differences. Nearest-valid extension supplies derivative support beside nodata. Plan curvature projects the Hessian along contours and profile curvature along the steepest slope. Saddles are low-gradient cells with an indefinite Hessian. These raster-scale proxies and crest lowering identify locations for investigation. Depression storage integrates filled-minus-original elevation over valid area. Compare terminal drainage locations between native and model grids, and flag native channel pixels with no downstream-network pixel in a one-model-cell neighbourhood. Flow inferred from a conditioned DEM cannot establish a dynamic inundation connection.

Raster inputs require a CRS. Projected north-up square metre grids retain their transform. Regional geographic or other nonmetric grids are warped to local metric UTM before analysis. Elevations default to metres when the band does not declare a unit. Declared feet are converted. Areas at partially valid edge cells are overlap-weighted. The output records CRS and nodata and writes both resolutions. The test suite includes a closed nine-cubic-metre pit, a plane, partial edge cells and a GeoTIFF with nodata.
