# Methods

## Governing equations

With water depth \(h\), bed \(z\), momentum \(\mathbf q=h\mathbf u\), rainfall rate \(R\) and infiltration \(I\), continuity is \(\partial_t h+\nabla\cdot\mathbf q=R-I\). The full momentum equations include flux \(\mathbf q\otimes\mathbf u+\tfrac12 gh^2\mathbf I\), bed force \(-gh\nabla z\) and Manning drag \(-g n^2|\mathbf q|\mathbf q/h^{7/3}\) for positive depth. Source integration preserves the configured mass and momentum treatment. LI evolves face-normal discharge with gravity and friction while omitting advective acceleration. It can therefore disagree with the full equations even with vanishing discretisation error. The independent reduced-equation dam and shear cases quantify this distinction. Contact-HLL FV1 uses hydrostatically cut piecewise-constant states and a forward Euler update. The low-Froude alternative changes numerical dissipation. MUSCL–Euler, Hancock and SSP-RK2 reconstruct cell states. Hancock predicts the reconstructed face states at half time and integrates one set of conservative face transports. RK2 forms two sets of fluxes and weights both accepted stages. Shock detection can revert local reconstructions to constant states. The same conservative flux crosses each shared face, including a wetting front. A separate cumulative ledger stores structure transfers when explicitly configured. All paper comparisons use FP64. ## Exact verification and synthetic reference

A frictional front travels with constant \(u\). With upstream distance \(D\), its depth satisfies \(dh/dD=n^2u^2/h^{4/3}-S\), where \(S\) is the positive downslope gradient. At zero grade \(h=(7 n^2u^2D/3)^{3/7}\) on the wet side. Volume averages integrate the wet fraction of a cell. For uniform downslope acceleration the velocity approaches \(u_\infty=h^{2/3}\sqrt{S}/n\). Gaussian shear translates in a uniform full-SWE flow, whereas LI lacks the advective term. The references for standing water, dam breaks, hydraulic jumps, steady equilibria and radial oscillation solve their corresponding continuous equations. Compare gauges to the time-integrated transport through the numerical face. The manufactured smooth benchmark separates space refinement from comparison to a much smaller step on a fixed mesh. ## Calibration, GPU cost and decision rule

Calibration searches a nested discrete grid around physical roughness. The discharge objective scales outlet RMSE by 0.01 m³/s. The spatial score is the root mean square of depth RMSE divided by 0.01 m and 1 cm inundation error divided by 0.10. The joint score uses the larger of flow and spatial scores. Select the smallest roughness change in a tie and hold the calibrated value fixed for transfer. The analytical archival comparison uses the original documented objectives. Group uncertainty at the independent realisation level. Several held-out combinations include the same physical event or fitted roughness. The RTX GPU runs one hydraulic experiment at a time. Timing controls are warmed and synchronised. Exact-release histories are collected in a separate replay after timing. Repeated timings are repeated observations of one configuration. The operational comparisons use production timestep policies. The 655,360-cell common-cap controls isolate per-step work. Process-pool usage includes retained Warp allocations, and allocator high-water is distinct from solver-owned buffers. The original timed loop, graph and per-kernel records accompany the event results. The advisor scales terrain and supplied pre-run storm descriptors by their development range, computes a normalised root-mean-square distance over shared features and retains independent nearest terrain realisations for each method. It uses the local median runtime, empirical error envelope and declared tolerance. Validation tunes the neighbour count, penalising false feasibility, missed feasible choices and regret in order. Final scoring holds out complete terrain realisations and the mixed family. Missing hydraulic descriptors widen the envelope. Domain, grid, hardware and support checks may produce abstentions. The selection applies to one formulation over the entire domain. Conditioned drainage is a terrain proxy. Actual wet fraction and Froude number are measured during a run.
