# Mixed-catchment recession

The native 10 m FV1 and tiled Hancock forecasts reached a common 20 h endpoint. The outlet and internal section passed the two-hour recession check. Finer reference calculations can still extend the end time for the full comparison.

| Phase | FV1 hydraulic time (s) | Hancock hydraulic time (s) | Hancock / FV1 | FV1 steps | Hancock steps | Hancock / FV1 per step |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rain | 23.21 | 43.57 | 1.88 | 25,045 | 39,846 | 1.18 |
| Early recession | 7.11 | 7.32 | 1.03 | 9,497 | 11,030 | 0.89 |
| Intermediate recession | 10.80 | 9.34 | 0.86 | 19,254 | 22,162 | 0.75 |
| Late recession | 16.22 | 14.25 | 0.88 | 32,050 | 42,645 | 0.66 |

Rain ends at hour 6. Early recession covers hours 6–8, intermediate recession hours 8–12 and late recession all subsequent hours. Each ratio divides the sum of measured runtimes for matched physical intervals.
