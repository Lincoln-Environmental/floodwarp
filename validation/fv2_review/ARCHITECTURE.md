# Architecture assessment before FV2 implementation

Base dev 8f15ad7. FV1 stores FP64 cell averages (depth, east and north discharge). Face kernels rotate to the face normal, construct wall/FREE/stage ghosts, cut both water columns at the higher bed, and apply contact-HLL. The update subtracts each side's reconstructed hydrostatic pressure before differencing. With constant cell states this gives exact still water. A linear cell profile also needs its within-cell surface gradient term.

FV1 selects a device timestep before splitting rain/infiltration, face transport, donor draining, Manning drag and optional disjoint culvert exchange. Rain integrates a piecewise-linear hyetograph; exponential capacity depends on cumulative withdrawal. Positive films retain water, with momentum suppressed below the dry threshold. CPU stepping and a device conditional CUDA graph share launches. Sparse FV1 schedules active tiles plus halos after sources, with dense storage. FV1 records a single accepted face flux, integrated face volumes, gross boundary volumes, accepted maxima, rain and soil histories. Checksummed checkpoints include the physical arrays and numerical identity. Direct-model changing stages currently use host interpolation.

FV2 can reuse native field validation, mask and boundary setup, physical ghost laws, contact-HLL, rainfall-knot selection, graph control, public getters and checkpoint IO. FV2 needs its own reconstruction, face CFL checks, bed forcing, stage states, source evolution, friction splitting, donor budget and RK-weighted accounting. Reusing FV1's complete Euler update twice would integrate the wrong sources and maxima. Culverts require a separately qualified second-order coupling. Dense indexing with exact dry-face omission is a possible first sparse stage; FV1's one-stage worklist cannot be reused across an entire RK step.

The archived Orton Bradley hour-25 checkpoint supplies a matched wet starting state at 5 m. Its historical “Purau” identifier refers to Orton Bradley. The upstream DEM provenance cites 1 m surveys. A solver solution on the archived 5 m grid alone cannot qualify convergence on source-resolution terrain.
